# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing the substance of an issue.
- Accept feedback gracefully and keep technical disagreement focused on evidence.
- Protect private, sensitive, and security-relevant information.
- Help make the project welcoming to first-time contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or sustained off-topic behavior.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's **Security** tab and
prefix the report title with `Conduct:`. If private reporting is unavailable,
open a minimal issue asking for a private contact channel; do not include
sensitive details. The maintainer will review reports promptly, protect the
reporter's privacy where possible, and take proportionate corrective action.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior that violates
this code. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
